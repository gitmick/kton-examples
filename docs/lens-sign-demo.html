Sign a claim about this figure

The figure below is checked against this instance's registry: the page fetches its bytes, SHA-256s them in your browser, and asks the instance whether it knows them. Nothing is uploaded.

Two buttons appear on a known figure. The round logo badge opens the provenance graph (read). The ✎ button signs a claim about it (write) — with a key generated in your browser as non-extractable, bound to your signed-in identity by a claim this instance signs.

What happens when you press ✎

  1. If you are not signed in, you are sent to Keycloak (which may broker you on to ORCID, GitHub or Google) and back — an OAuth2 authorization-code flow with PKCE. Keycloak rather than Bonfire, because only Keycloak passes the ORCID iD through.
  2. On first use your browser generates an Ed25519 key with extractable: false and registers only the public half. The instance signs a sec:controller claim binding that key to your ORCID iD, if it has one.
  3. The kton WASM kernel canonicalises the claim and frames the bytes to sign; WebCrypto makes the signature; the kernel reassembles the DSSE envelope.
  4. The finished record is filed. It is byte-identical to the same claim signed by the nekton CLI.
